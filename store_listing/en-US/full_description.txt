Multi Pong is a local two-player arcade game where each player holds their own Android phone. Place the phones side by side and a single play field spans both screens — the asteroid flies from one phone to the other as if the screens are one.

HOW TO PLAY
Each player controls a satellite paddle on their own phone by sliding their finger. Bounce the asteroid past your opponent's paddle to score. First to the target score wins.

SPACE THEME
The asteroid tumbles and rotates in flight, its fiery tail always trailing opposite to its direction. Paddles are pixel-art satellites with glowing solar panels. Everything plays out against a deep-space starfield.

CONNECTING
Two methods — no internet required, everything is local:
• Bluetooth scan: one player hosts, the other scans and taps the host in the list.
• NFC tap: tap the two phones back-to-back for instant pairing without scanning.

LOBBY SETTINGS (host configures before the game starts)
• Ball speed — how fast the asteroid starts
• Speed gain — how much faster the asteroid gets after each hit
• Win score — first to how many points wins

CROSS-DEVICE PLAY FIELD
The game measures both screens and uses the smallest common height so the play field is physically the same size on both phones regardless of resolution or screen size. A dashed alignment guide helps you line up the two phones at the correct height.

No accounts. No ads. No internet. Just you, a friend, and two phones.
