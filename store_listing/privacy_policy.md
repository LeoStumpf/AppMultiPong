# Privacy Policy — Multi Pong by SpaceHats

_Last updated: May 2026_

## Summary

Multi Pong does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no internet connection is used during gameplay.

---

## What the app does

Multi Pong connects two Android phones locally using **Bluetooth** or **NFC** so two players can share a single game session. All communication happens directly between the two devices on the local radio link; no data leaves the devices.

## Data collected

**None.** The app does not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or any account information
- Location data (Bluetooth scan permission is declared for Android API < 31 compatibility only; the app never calls location APIs)
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Crash reports

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `BLUETOOTH`, `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN` | Initiate and manage Bluetooth connections (API ≤ 30) |
| `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` | Discover and connect to nearby devices (API 31+) |
| `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | Required by Android OS for Bluetooth scan on API ≤ 30; no location data is read or stored |
| `NFC` | Optional tap-to-pair — delivers the host's Bluetooth address to the client without manual scanning |

## Third-party services

None. The app has no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or ad networks.

## Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
